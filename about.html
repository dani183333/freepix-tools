About Freepix Tools

Welcome to Freepix Tools, a simple online platform designed to make everyday image editing easier.

Our goal is to provide accessible tools that help users compress images, resize photos, and convert image formats without installing additional software.

Our Tools

  • Image Compressor: adjust image quality and reduce file size.
  • Image Resizer: change image dimensions to suit your needs.
  • Image Converter: convert supported images between common formats.

Privacy and Simplicity

Freepix Tools is designed for straightforward image processing in your browser. Please review our privacy policy for more information about data handling.
